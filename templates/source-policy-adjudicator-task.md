# Source-policy adjudication packet

Resolve only the source-level facts and the independent A/B results supplied
here. Do not infer rights from a license name alone. Any unresolved legal or
factual uncertainty remains blocked.

Allowed outcomes:

- `embedded_public`
- `external_ref_only`
- `exclude_public`
- `needs_human_legal_review`

Emit a recommendation with rationale. Maintainer approval is a separate,
explicit step and is never implied by this task.
